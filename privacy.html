Privacy

23 October 2026

Stereo Dandifier collects no information about its users, and sends nothing anywhere. It makes no network connections, contains no analytics or advertising, and does not track its users.

Photographs

The photographs the operator stages remain where they lie, and are read only on the Mac. A collection records where each file is, and the settings made for it; it is saved wherever the operator chooses. Pictures the app develops for speed are kept in the app's own cache folder on the Mac, and may be deleted at any time.

Captions

Should the operator ask the Text & Order node to write captions ("Fill Blanks"), the pictures are described by Apple Intelligence's on-device model, upon the Mac itself.

Preferences

Window positions, appearance and export preferences are stored on the Mac, in the app's own preferences.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to harris_stereography@icloud.com.
