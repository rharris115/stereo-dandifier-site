Stereo Dandifier · Help · Support · Privacy

Support

Stereo Dandifier prepares stereo photographs for viewing, from the camera's own files to the finished picture or stereo card. It requires a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26.5 or later.

Help

Every node's card bears an ⓘ, which explains it. The whole manual is under Help › Stereo Dandifier Help in the app, and here, online.

Questions and faults

Questions, and reports of faults, may be sent to harris_stereography@icloud.com. It is helpful to state the camera that made the files, the macOS version, and what was done before the fault appeared.
