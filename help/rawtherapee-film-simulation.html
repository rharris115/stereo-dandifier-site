RawTherapee Film Simulation

The look of a particular film stock, from RawTherapee's Film Simulation collection (CC BY-SA 4.0, acknowledged in the app's Credits). The menu follows the collection's own arrangement: Color and Black-and-White, by maker.

Settings

The same for every pair, and saved with the workflow:

  • Film — Kodak Kodachrome 64 Generic by default.
  • Intensity — how much of the look is applied over the original.

The dialog charts the film's characteristic curve: the density it gives each channel, by exposure.

With black-and-white film it is advisable to place a Wratten Lens Filter before it, to govern how colours are rendered as greys.
