Side-by-Side

Reads files that are each a single frame with both eyes side by side, and divides each down its middle: the left half for the left eye, the right half for the right.

Which files come here

The loader is made automatically for files of a format and camera that no loader serves, when either:

  • the lens is a stereoscopic one — its name containing DUAL FISHEYE, as with Canon's RF 5.2mm and RF-S 3.9mm Dual Fisheye lenses; or
  • the camera says so in its own tags — as the Kandao QooCam EGO does.

It then takes every staged file of that format and camera. RAW files go on to a RAW Develop of this loader's own. A dual fisheye may then be undone by Defish.

A file is counted on the badge once it has been examined in the background: green in good order, amber with a warning, red if it cannot be read. See Badges and Marks.

Actions

  • Unstage All — withdraws its files from the collection; the loader goes with them.
