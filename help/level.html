Level

Rotates both eyes of a pair together, through the same angle about the same centre, to level its horizon. The roll between the eyes is left as Stereo Align made it. Both are then cropped so that no blank corner remains.

Settings for each pair

  • Level — up to 15° either way, clockwise positive. Type it, or turn it 0.05° at a press with the buttons either side; hold a button down to keep turning.
  • Horizontal Guide and Vertical Guide — a line across both eyes, to be dragged to a horizon or an upright; rotate until the two agree. They are aids only, and are not kept.

A level set before something upstream was altered is marked "Upstream changed" until it is set again.

Where it goes

After Stereo Align. Rotated first, part of the pair's depth would stand vertically, beyond Align's power to remove, and every change of level would put Align's measurement out of date.

A pair taken with the camera tilted cannot be levelled entirely without cost: rotation sets a little of the depth on the vertical, the more so the greater the angle and the nearer the subject. A small correction will be found comfortable to view.

Actions

  • Reset All — returns every pair to no rotation.
