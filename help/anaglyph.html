Anaglyph

Makes each pair a single picture for coloured spectacles, at the eyes' own resolution. It ends a branch: its pictures leave the workflow through Export… and Print…, on its card and in its dialog.

Settings

The same for every pair, and saved with the workflow.

  • Glasses — chosen on the card, to match the spectacles: Red / Cyan (the default), Green / Magenta or Amber / Blue.
  • Number — where each pair's number is printed: Top Centre or a top corner, in a band above the picture; a bottom corner, on the caption's line; or None (the default). It requires a Text & Order node before this one, which also sets the order of export; without one it is disabled.

Method

How each eye's colours are mixed into the channels its filter admits:

  • Colour — each eye keeps its own colours: the fullest colour, though strong colours may contend between the eyes.
  • Half Colour (the default) — one eye sees brightness alone: less contention, most of the colour kept.
  • Grey — both eyes see brightness alone: no colour, the least contention.
  • Dubois — a mixture fitted to the spectacles' filters: the least ghosting, at some cost in colour. See the Dubois method.
