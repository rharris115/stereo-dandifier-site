Crop

Frames each pair. One rectangle is cut identically from both eyes, so the depth is left exactly as it was.

Settings for each pair

  • Drag the rectangle's edges or corners in either eye.
  • Aspect ratio — 1:1, 5:4, 4:3, 3:2 or 16:9, upright or oblong. Choosing one fits the largest rectangle of that shape within the crop.

A crop is at least 5% of the picture each way. It is held as a share of the picture, so it is the same at whatever size the pair is shown or exported.

A near object cut by the edge of the frame will be found uncomfortable to view; see The Stereo Window.

Actions

  • Reset All — returns every pair to the whole picture.
