Side-by-Side

Lays out each pair as one picture with the eyes side by side, at the eyes' own resolution. It ends a branch: its pictures leave the workflow through Export… (the Save panel, with format and size among its options) and Print…, on its card and in its dialog.

Settings

The same for every pair, and saved with the workflow:

  • Full width — each eye whole, for free viewing or a viewer.
  • Half width — each eye squeezed to half its width, so the picture is as wide as one eye, as 3D televisions and headsets expect.
  • Parallel — the left eye on the left.
  • Cross-eyed — the eyes exchanged, for cross-eyed viewing.
  • Number — where each pair's number is printed: Top Centre or a top corner, in a band above the picture; a bottom corner, on the caption's line; or None (the default). It requires a Text & Order node before this one, which also sets the order of export; without one it is disabled.
