Adjust Colour

Preview's colour controls, set for each pair. For JPEG and MPO pairs, which never pass through RAW Develop, this is the only place to set white balance.

Settings for each pair

  • Exposure — up to 6 stops either way: ample to make good a deep filter earlier in the workflow.
  • Contrast — about mid-grey.
  • Highlights — brings bright passages down. Shadows — lifts dark ones.
  • Saturation — from grey to doubled. Vibrance — strengthens subdued colours, sparing those already strong.
  • Temperature — warmer or cooler, in steps that appear equal. Tint — towards magenta or green.
  • Levels — applied last of all: the black point becomes black, the white point white, and the mid-tone bends the tones between. Auto Levels sets the black and white points at the extremes of the picture's tones.

Each group has a Reset of its own, and the histograms show truly what is clipped.

Should something earlier in the workflow be altered after a pair was adjusted, the pair is marked amber, that it may be examined again.

Actions

  • Auto Level All — sets every pair's black and white points at the extremes of its tones.
  • Reset All — returns every pair to neutral.
