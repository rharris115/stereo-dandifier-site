LUT

Applies a look table of the operator's own: a creative .cube file, or a HaldCLUT image (PNG or TIFF). Until one is imported, pictures pass through unaltered. The table is kept within the workflow, so a saved .sdworkflow carries it to another Mac.

Settings

The same for every pair, and saved with the workflow:

  • Import… — chooses the table.
  • Intensity — how much of the look is applied over the original.

Log-conversion LUTs, made for video recorded in a log profile, will not give a satisfactory result: ordinary colour pictures are expected.
