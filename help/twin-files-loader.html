Twin Files

Reads pairs made of two files, one for each eye, matched by name — as a two-camera rig writes them: a GoPro pair's 3D_L0255 and 3D_R0255, or an MPO divided into DSCF1267_L and DSCF1267_R.

How files are matched

A file's name must mark its eye once: L, R, Left or Right, standing as a part of the name by itself, in capitals or not. Two files whose names agree in every other respect are the two eyes of one exposure. A name with no such mark, or more than one, goes to Match Images.

Which files come here

The loader is made automatically when both eyes' files of at least one exposure, of a format and camera that no loader serves, are staged. It then takes every staged file of that format and camera.

A file whose fellow has not been staged is listed red, and goes no further until its partner is staged or it is unstaged. A pair is counted on the badge once its files have been examined in the background: green in good order, amber with a warning, red if it cannot be read. See Badges and Marks.

Actions

  • Unstage All — withdraws its files from the collection; the loader goes with them.
