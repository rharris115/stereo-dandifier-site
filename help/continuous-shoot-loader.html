Continuous Shoot

For a single camera moved sideways between exposures in continuous drive. Frames taken close together form a sequence, and two frames of each sequence become its pair.

Which files come here

The loader is made automatically when two files of one format and camera, served by no other loader, were taken within a second of each other. It then takes every staged file of that format and camera. A frame belonging to no sequence goes to Match Images, and returns should a frame taken close to it be staged.

RAW files go on to a RAW Develop of this loader's own.

Settings

The same for every sequence, and saved with the workflow:

  • Gap — frames no further apart than this form one sequence. One second by default: continuous drive takes its frames a fraction of a second apart, whereas composing the next view takes several. Frames left out by a narrower gap go to Match Images.
  • Infinity disparity — the depth Find Pair aims for: the disparity of the infinity points, as a share of the picture's width. The default, 3.3%, is the customary rule of one-thirtieth.

Choosing each sequence's pair

Click a frame to make it one of the eyes. Lock a frame, and each click then replaces the other. Find Pair measures the sequence and chooses the two frames nearest the target disparity, or the best partner for a locked frame. Until a choice is made, a sequence's pair is its first two frames.

A sequence of only two frames has no pair to choose, only which frame is the left eye, which depends on the direction the camera moved. It is therefore measured in the background, unasked, and set the right way round.

Actions

  • Find Pairs — measures every sequence not yet measured, or wanting a pair, and gives each the pair its measurement suggests.
  • Reset All — returns every sequence to its first two frames.
  • Unstage All — withdraws its files from the collection; the loader goes with them.
