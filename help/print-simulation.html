Print Simulation

The look of historical printing processes: silver gelatin, the sepias, albumen, selenium, cyanotype and others. These are StereoDandifier's own looks, tuned by eye, not measured from actual prints.

Settings

The same for every pair, and saved with the workflow:

  • Print — Silver Gelatin by default.
  • Intensity — how much of the look is applied over the original.

The dialog charts the print's characteristic curve: the density it gives each channel, by exposure.
